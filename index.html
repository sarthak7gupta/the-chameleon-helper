◉ the chameleon.

3–8 friends. One very good liar. Dice & topic helper

The art of blending in.

  1. Keep your card private. Everyone gets the same secret word, except one player: the Chameleon.
  2. One word each. Starting with the dealer, give a clue related to the secret. Too obvious and you help the Chameleon. Repeated clues are allowed.
  3. Talk it out, then vote. Discuss clues in person or on a voice call. The most votes decides the accused; the dealer breaks a tie.
  4. One last chance. A caught Chameleon gets one guess at the word, or two with three players. With 7–8 players, the topic disappears after clues.

Escape: Chameleon +2. Caught but guesses correctly: Chameleon +1. Caught and fails: everyone else +2. First to 5 wins; tied winners share the win. The Chameleon deals next.
